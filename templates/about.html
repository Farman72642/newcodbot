🌊 About Water Remover App

Water Remover App is a powerful web-based tool designed to help you remove watermarks and unwanted elements from your images. Using advanced image processing algorithms, our application can intelligently detect and remove watermarks while preserving the quality of your original image.

✨ Features

  • 🎯 Multiple Processing Methods: Choose from Inpaint, Gaussian Blur, or Median Filter
  • 🚀 Fast Processing: Get your processed images in seconds
  • 🔒 Privacy First: Images are processed locally and not stored on servers
  • 📱 Responsive Design: Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile devices
  • 🎨 Multiple Format Support: PNG, JPG, JPEG, GIF, and BMP
  • 💾 Easy Download: Download your processed images instantly

🛠️ Technology Stack

Python Flask OpenCV Pillow NumPy

📖 How to Use

  1. Click on "Home" in the navigation menu
  2. Select an image file from your device
  3. Choose your preferred processing method
  4. Click "Remove Watermark" button
  5. Download your processed image

⚠️ Disclaimer

This tool is intended for educational purposes and for removing watermarks from your own images or images where you have the legal right to do so. Please respect copyright laws and intellectual property rights when using this application.
